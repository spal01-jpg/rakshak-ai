READY 4s

Post-Patrol Muscle Relaxation (PMR)

Physical Recovery

Step-by-step neuromuscular decompression to eliminate physical tension stored during long patrols.

1
Lower Extremities (Feet & Calves)

Curl your toes downward, tighten your calves for 5 seconds, then breathe out and completely release.

2
Thighs & Lower Back

Tighten your thigh muscles and press your lower back firmly into the bed/ground. Hold 5s, release.

3
Shoulders & Heavy Vest Release

Hunch your shoulders all the way to your ears to relieve patrol body armor weight. Hold 5s, drop shoulders loose.

4
Hands & Forearms

Clench your fists tightly as if holding gear. Hold for 5 seconds, then unclench and spread your fingers wide.
